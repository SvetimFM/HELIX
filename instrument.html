Declared semantic limits

A note's pitch is computed from stance, valence, and abstraction. Musical mappings are artistic conventions, not demonstrated universal semantics. A score round-trips exactly because we retain symbolic metadata, not because raw sound can reveal hidden intentions. Possible futures coexist without being assigned probabilities or declared true.
